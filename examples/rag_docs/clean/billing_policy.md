# Billing Policy

## Plans

RAGGuard offers a Free tier, a Pro tier, and an Enterprise tier. The Free tier
includes 100 documents and 1,000 questions per month. The Pro tier raises these to
5,000 documents and 50,000 questions per month.

## Refunds

Pro subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. A prorated refund is issued for the
unused portion of the current billing period. Enterprise contracts follow the terms
in the signed agreement.

## Overage

If a project exceeds its monthly question quota, additional questions are billed at
the per-question overage rate listed on the pricing page. Administrators receive an
email warning at 80% and 100% of quota.
